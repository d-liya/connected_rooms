# Active game brief

This checkout is the neutral template, not a completed game. Demonstrate a title screen,
skippable intro, horizontal movement, two connected rooms, pause/resume, and restart.
There are no enemies, objectives, or win/loss rules until a generated brief supplies them.

For generation, replace this document with the intended story, primary interaction,
progression, success/failure rules, and presentation requirements. Assets and geometry
are supplied in src/generated/*.json. Keep their IDs and calibrated metadata intact.
